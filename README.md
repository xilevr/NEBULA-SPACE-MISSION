🌌 NEBULA: Space Mission Design Game

You’re the flight director. Every gram, watt and credit counts.

NEBULA turns real space-engineering decisions into a game. Pick a mission, build your spacecraft, and find out if it survives.

🛠️ Design: balance mass, power, propellant and budget under real constraints

🧠 Neuron Core: an in-browser AI that diagnoses your design and suggests improvements

🪐 Orbital Lab: Hohmann transfers, Tsiolkovsky Δv and eclipse cycles, computed live

🔬 Science Lab: choose instruments and maximize the data you bring home

⚡ Mission Simulator: solar storms, comms blackouts and power failures, with decisions that matter

📊 Mission Analytics: see exactly why you succeeded or failed

👯 Twin Mode: two designs, the same random events, a fair comparison

Built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge. It runs offline from a single HTML file, and a companion Colab notebook proves every equation numerically. 🚀
